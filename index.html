THE TRUST PROBLEM

More information. Not always more certainty.

Fake opportunities, misleading claims, unreliable services and incomplete information make everyday decisions harder. TrustNet helps organize available evidence, identify uncertainty and explain what is known and what still needs verification.

TRUST INTELLIGENCE

Evidence over assumptions.

TrustNet is designed to distinguish between verified facts, independent evidence, user-submitted claims, unverified reports and AI-generated interpretations. An allegation is not proof. A high rating is not automatic verification. Context matters.
