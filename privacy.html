Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information TrustNet may collect, why it is used, how it is protected and what choices you have.

Effective date: November 6, 2026 Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. Introduction

TrustNet respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information may be collected, used, stored, shared and protected when you access our website, applications and services.

The information we process depends on the features you use, your account settings, your interactions with other users and applicable law.

2. Information We Collect

A. Account Information

When you register or manage an account, we may collect:

  • Full name and username.
  • Email address.
  • Phone number and country calling code.
  • Country or general location information you provide.
  • Profile photograph and biography.
  • Account preferences and settings.

B. Private Profile Information

Depending on the information requested by a feature, we may process details such as date of birth, gender or phone number. These details should not be publicly displayed unless the feature requires it and you have been informed and given the necessary choice or consent.

C. User-Submitted Content

We may collect information you submit, including:

  • Trust assessments and supporting evidence.
  • Reviews, ratings, reports and disputes.
  • Business, product and service listings.
  • Messages, attachments and project information.
  • Community posts, comments and reactions.
  • Questions, AI prompts and feedback.

D. Technical and Usage Information

We may collect technical information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Browser type and device information.
  • Operating system and application version.
  • Login times, session information and security events.
  • Pages, features and actions used on the platform.
  • Error logs and performance information.

3. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create, authenticate and manage user accounts.
  • Provide profiles and account settings.
  • Process trust assessments and evidence submissions.
  • Operate discovery and marketplace features.
  • Enable messaging, projects and community features.
  • Generate AI-assisted analysis and recommendations.
  • Send essential service and security notifications.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, spam and unauthorized access.
  • Investigate disputes and enforce platform rules.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot and improve the service.
  • Meet legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Legal Grounds for Processing

Where required by applicable privacy law, we process personal information on one or more lawful grounds, which may include:

  • Your consent.
  • Performance of a contract or requested service.
  • Compliance with legal obligations.
  • Legitimate interests, where permitted by law.
  • Protection of vital interests where legally applicable.

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent, subject to legal limitations and the consequences explained when the information was collected.

5. Public and Private Information

TrustNet may have public profiles, listings, reviews and community content alongside private account information.

Public information may include profile names, usernames, photographs, biographies, business listings and content you choose to publish.

Private information may include login-related information, private contact details, date of birth, account security information and private messages.

We aim to restrict private account information to authorized purposes and access. However, information you intentionally publish or share with other users may be copied, saved or redistributed by those users.

Do not publish sensitive personal information unless you understand the audience and consequences.

6. Trust Assessments and Personal Information

TrustNet may process information about people, businesses and other entities when users request assessments or submit supporting information.

Such information may include user claims, public information, reviews, submitted documents, reported concerns and assessment explanations.

We aim to distinguish allegations from independently verified information. A user-submitted allegation is not automatically treated as an established fact.

If information about you is inaccurate, misleading or improperly associated with you, you may contact us to request correction, clarification or review, subject to applicable law.

7. Artificial Intelligence and Automated Processing

TrustNet may use AI systems to analyze submitted information, summarize evidence, compare options, generate recommendations and assist with platform operations.

Depending on the feature and its technical implementation, information you submit may be processed by AI service providers acting on our behalf.

AI-generated output may be inaccurate or incomplete. We encourage human review, particularly where an output may affect another person's reputation or an important decision.

We will provide additional information or choices where applicable law requires disclosures or safeguards for automated decision-making.

8. When We Share Information

We do not sell personal information to advertisers. We may share information in limited circumstances, including:

A. With Other Users

Information you publish, submit to a public area or intentionally share with another user may be visible to that audience.

B. Service Providers

We may use service providers for hosting, authentication, database services, file storage, security, communications, analytics and AI processing.

C. Legal and Safety Requirements

We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to respond to lawful requests, investigate abuse, protect users or defend legal rights.

D. Business Changes

Information may be transferred as part of a merger, restructuring, acquisition or transfer of business assets, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

9. Third-Party Services

TrustNet may rely on third-party services, including Firebase infrastructure, hosting providers and AI services.

These providers may process information under their own terms, privacy policies and contractual obligations. Their processing is not entirely controlled by TrustNet.

We encourage users to review the relevant third-party privacy information where appropriate.

10. International Data Transfers

TrustNet may operate using service providers or infrastructure located in different countries. Your information may therefore be transferred to or processed outside your country.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers.

11. Data Security

We aim to protect information through appropriate technical and organizational measures. These may include:

  • Authentication and access controls.
  • Restricted access to private information.
  • Secure transmission where supported.
  • Security monitoring and abuse detection.
  • Backups and operational safeguards.
  • Access reviews and security improvements.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their passwords and report suspected account compromise.

12. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain account records, resolve disputes, enforce policies and meet legal obligations.

Retention periods depend on the type of information, the purpose for which it was collected and applicable law.

Some information may remain in backups or security records for a limited period after deletion from active systems.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of certain information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Request a portable copy of certain information.
  • Complain to the relevant data protection authority.

These rights may be subject to legal exceptions. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

14. Cookies and Similar Technologies

TrustNet may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to support login sessions, remember preferences, maintain security and understand how the service is used.

Browser settings may allow you to manage or block certain cookies. Some functionality may stop working if essential storage technologies are disabled.

Where legally required, we will request consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

15. Children's Privacy

TrustNet is not intended to collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection would be unlawful.

Where a user is below the age at which they can legally consent to data processing, any required parent or guardian authorization must be obtained.

If we learn that personal information has been collected unlawfully from a child, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

16. Account Deletion

You may request account deletion through available account settings or by contacting us.

Deletion may remove or disable access to your profile and associated services. Certain records may be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention or dispute resolution.

Public content already copied or redistributed by other users may not be fully recoverable.

17. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services, technology or legal obligations change.

The latest version will be published on this page. Where required, we will provide notice of significant changes and obtain consent where necessary.
